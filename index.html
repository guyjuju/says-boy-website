Everything in the menu

Each card below is one tab of the in-game menu, in the same order you see it.

Player tab 1

  • Walk speed and jump power sliders
  • Infinite jump and NoClip
  • Flight with a speed slider, Shift to boost and E to fly straight up
  • On-screen Fly button for phones
  • Camera and tool options

Wood tab 2

  • Auto Bring Wood: pick a wood type and press Get Selected Tree
  • Mod Wood with auto sell
  • Mod Sawmill and sawmill sizes
  • Axe Options
  • Works with the Silver axe and on Xeno

Auto Buy tab 3

  • Pick an item and an amount, and it buys and delivers them
  • Misc buy options

Items tab 4

  • Item Stacker: tap where the stack goes and set the width, length and height
  • Group Select: select every item of one kind, then bring or stack them
  • Auto Open Boxes for the player you pick
  • Click to Unbox, Far Axe Equip, Hard Dragger and Count Items
  • Max Land, Infinite Wire and Tp Gifts
  • A Paint tool to re-place structures in another wood

Blueprints tab 5

  • Get All Blueprints
  • Fill Blueprints with the wood you choose, in any size

Vehicle tab 6

  • Vehicle options
  • Car Spawner

Troll tab 7

  • Claim All Trees: one hit on every tree except Lone Cave
  • Auto Chop Nearby with a radius slider
  • Spin, Lay Down, Jump Spam, Ragdoll, Headless and Fake Lag

World tab 8

  • Time of day and visual options
  • Map options
  • Teleports to stores, the dock, the volcano, the ski lodge and more

Settings tab 9

  • UI Scale from 50% to 150%
  • Mobile UI toggle and Fly button
  • Close Script to stop everything and remove the menu

Built for phones too

Sliders respond to touch, the stacker and Group Select work with taps, and there is a floating button to open and close the menu.
